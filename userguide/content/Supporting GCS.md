# Supporting GCS

GCS is free and open source, and it’s a spare-time project of its author, Richard Wilkes. Sponsors help keep it that
way. If you find GCS useful, you can support its development with a monthly sponsorship or a one-time donation.

## Become a sponsor

A sponsorship is a monthly contribution made through GitHub Sponsors. It starts at $1 a month, and you need a GitHub
account.

1. Go to **Help > Sponsor GCS Development**. The [GitHub Sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/richardwilkes) for
   GCS opens in your browser.
2. Choose a monthly amount, then follow the steps on that page.

You can also select **Sponsor GCS Development** in the **About GCS** window, or **Sponsor GCS** in the window that
tells you about a [new version](Installing%20and%20Updating%20GCS#updating-gcs).

### What sponsors get

- A sponsor role in the [GCS Discord community](https://discord.gg/MKhuDPPagY).
- Access to the private sponsors channel there.
- More say in what gets built next.

> [!TIP]
> To claim your sponsor role, type `/link` in any channel of the GCS Discord and follow the steps the bot sends you.

## Make a one-time donation

If you’d rather not commit to a monthly amount, go to **Help > Make a One-time Donation for GCS Development**. The
[PayPal page](https://paypal.me/GURPSCharacterSheet) for GCS opens in your browser.

## Other ways to help

- Report bugs and request features by [creating an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/richardwilkes/gcs/issues).
- Add to or correct the Master Library. See
  [Contributing to the Master Library](Contributing%20to%20the%20Master%20Library).
- Help other users in the [GCS Discord community](https://discord.gg/MKhuDPPagY).
