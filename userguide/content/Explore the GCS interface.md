# Explore the GCS Interface

When you open GCS, you’ll see its main window with three key areas that you’ll use often:

- **Menu bar:** Your main control center for commands and tools.
- **Library Explorer:** A hub for browsing and managing your libraries and files.
- **Document Workspace:** Where your character sheets, lists, and other documents appear while you work on them.

## Menu bar

The Menu bar runs along the top of GCS and contains menus for most of the actions you can take.

It includes:

- **File:** Create new sheets, templates, libraries, and other files (**File > New**), open, save, export, or print.
- **Edit:** Undo, redo, cut, copy, paste, duplicate, delete, select all, and jump to the search or filter field.
- **Insert:** Add new traits, skills, spells, equipment, notes, and attacks to the document you're working on.
- **Item:** Act on the selected items: open the detail editor, open page references, move, adjust, or convert items,
  toggle their state, apply modifiers, copy them to a sheet or template, and sync them with their library source.
- **Sheet:** Commands for the active character sheet, such as applying a template, organizing traits, buying and
  selling equipment, showing the sheet as pages, and the sheet's own settings, attributes, body type, and layout.
- **Settings:** General settings, colors, fonts, menu keys, saved filters, the defaults for new sheets, and page
  reference mappings. See [General Settings](General%20Settings) and the other pages under **Customization**.
- **View:** Scale the active document up or down, and open the [Calculators](Calculator).
- **Window:** Manage how windows and tabs are arranged, including undocking a tab into its own window.
- **Help:** Open the User Guide, check for updates, read the release notes, and visit the GCS website.

> [!TIP]
> Most commands have keyboard shortcuts, shown beside them in the menus. You can change them in [Menu
> Keys](Menu%20Keys). Right-clicking a row in a list shows the commands that apply to it.

## Library Explorer

The [Library Explorer](Library%20Explorer) appears in the left sidebar and displays folders containing traits, skills,
spells, and equipment, among other content.

It contains:

- **Favorites:** Files and folders you've marked as favorites for quick access.
- **User Library:** Your personal collection of custom content and character sheets.
- **Master Library:** The official library of lists aligned with GURPS books.

The **Library Explorer toolbar** gives you buttons to expand or collapse folders, create or configure a library, update
the Master Library, manage folders and files, and mark favorites. Its search field finds files by name, and when a new
version of GCS is available a **Software Update Available** button appears here too.

For more details, see [Library Explorer](Library%20Explorer).

## Document Workspace

The Document Workspace is where everything you open in GCS appears: character sheets, templates, lists, editors, PDFs,
and settings.

### Windows

Each document opens in its own **window** inside the workspace.

You can:

- Resize windows by dragging the dividers.
- Scroll horizontally or vertically within a window.
- Maximize a window to fill the whole workspace:
  - Select **Maximize** ![maximize](images/icons/icn-windowMaximize.svg) in the top-right corner of the window.
  - Select **Restore** ![restore](images/icons/icn-windowRestore.svg) to return to the side-by-side view.

### Tabs

When you open multiple documents, each appears in its own **tab** within a window and has its own **toolbar** for
relevant actions, such as searching and filtering. Tabs make it easy to switch between documents without closing them.

- **Unsaved Changes:** An asterisk (`*`) in a tab's name means the document has unsaved changes. Save to remove the
  asterisk.
- **Rearranging Tabs:** Drag tabs to reorder them within a window.
- **Moving Tabs Between Windows:** Drag a tab to another window to group documents together.
- **Splitting into New Windows:** Drag a tab away from the current window to create a separate one.
- **Docking Options:** While dragging, a highlight shows where the window will be placed: side-by-side, top, bottom, or
  any combination. This lets you customize your workspace layout.
- **Undocking:** Select **Window > Undock From Workspace** to move the current tab into a window of its own, outside the
  main GCS window.

When a window is too narrow for a document's toolbar, the toolbar wraps onto more rows, with the search field filling
the rest of the last row.

> [!TIP]
> Quickly close tabs by right-clicking a tab:
>
> - **Close Other Tabs** closes all tabs except the one you clicked.
> - **Close All Tabs** closes every open tab in the window, including the one you clicked. This does not quit GCS.

> [!NOTE]
> With **Restore arrangement on start** turned on in [General Settings](General%20Settings), GCS reopens the documents
> you had open, in the same arrangement, the next time it starts.

## Keyboard and screen readers

GCS works with screen readers (VoiceOver on macOS, Narrator, NVDA, and JAWS on Windows, and Orca on Linux) and can be
driven from the keyboard:

- Press **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** to move between controls.
- In a list, use the arrow keys to move between rows. Press the right arrow on a row with nothing left to open to move
  into its cells, where **Space** activates what the cell holds (such as a page reference or a check mark) and
  **Escape** returns to the row.
- Press **Shift+F10** or the **Menu** key to open a list's context menu.
- Links, such as page references, can be followed with **Space** or **Return** once they have the focus.
